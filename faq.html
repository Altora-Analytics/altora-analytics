The basics

What is Altora Analytics?
A set of statistical models that publish calibrated win probabilities for football, tennis, darts, snooker, rugby, esports, basketball and handball — plus systematic trading systems — with every prediction published before the event and settled publicly. The records are the product: nothing is deleted, ever.
Are these betting tips?
No. We publish analysis — model probabilities — not instructions to bet. Some of our tracked records simulate fixed-stake betting so you can judge the models honestly, but nothing here is financial advice. If you bet, that's your decision and your responsibility. 18+ only, and please read BeGambleAware.org.
How do the models actually work?
The core is a player/team rating system (an adaptive Elo) blended with the market's own opening price through a gradient-boosted calibration layer, validated on tens of thousands of historical matches it never trained on. Football uses a larger feature set including expected-goals signals. The full write-up is on the methodology page — and the complete build method is in our handbooks.

The honesty questions

Do you ever delete losing picks?
Never. Every record page shows wins and losses, and offers the complete history as a CSV download — date, selection, odds, result. A deleted loser would leave a hole in the CSV and a mismatch with the posts we've already published. That verifiability is the whole point of the site.
Why do you show the bookmaker beating your model sometimes?
Because it's true. Betting markets at closing are extraordinarily efficient — matching them is genuinely hard, and claiming to smash them is the oldest lie in this industry. Our models are calibrated (when we say 70%, it happens about 70% of the time) and statistically level with the market on most sports. We show the comparison either way.
Will I make money following the models?
We make no such promise, and you should distrust anyone who does. Bookmaker margins mean most bettors lose over time. What we offer is honest, calibrated information and fully transparent records — what you do with it is up to you.

Data & sources

Where does the data come from?
API-Sports (football, rugby union, basketball, handball), Betfair Exchange (live market prices and results), football-data.co.uk (historical football odds), tennis-data.co.uk (historical tennis results and odds) and OpenDota (Dota 2). Each sport's record page credits its own sources.
Can I buy your data?
The raw feeds aren't ours to sell — they belong to the sources above. Our own model outputs and published records are freely downloadable from every record page as CSV.
