How to read this honestly

Our picks are calibrated probabilities, not tips, and the exchange prices they face are sharp: a model that merely matches the market loses the spread here, which is the honest baseline to expect early. If a sport sustains a positive level-stakes ROI as its sample grows past a few hundred picks, that is evidence of real edge; until then this page is a stress test, not a boast. The accuracy view lives on each sport's record page, the market-movement view on the closing line value page.
