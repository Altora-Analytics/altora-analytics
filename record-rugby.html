Two codes, two different models

Union and league are validated and published separately, because they are built differently. Union uses full match scores and stable team identifiers from a sports-data API, so its ratings incorporate winning margins and recent scoring form. League (NRL, Super League) has no such feed — its ratings are built from exchange results, which record only who won. That is why the two accuracies differ, and we would rather show you two honest numbers than one blended one. On both codes the market is sharper than the model — it sees team news we can't — and we say so openly.
