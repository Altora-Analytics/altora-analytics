Sorting by it puts the fixtures where we most differ from the market at the top, because those are the interesting ones — but our own research found that wider disagreement in these markets is if anything less reliable, not more. How to read this. The big number is the model's probability; the small number beneath it is the market's price, shown so you can see the two side by side. Kick-off times are UK. Nothing here is a tip and nothing here is a value call — our own studies found the model beats the market on accuracy in these markets while still losing to the bookmaker's margin, so treat these as forecasts, not selections.
● marks a season opener.
