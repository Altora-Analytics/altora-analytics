“vs mkt” is disagreement, not value. It is the model's probability minus the market's, once the bookmaker's margin is stripped out, in percentage points. Sorting by it puts the fixtures where we most differ from the market at the top, because those are the interesting ones — but our own research found that wider disagreement in these markets is if anything less reliable, not more. A big number here means we disagree, and nothing else.
⚑ marks a big disagreement — the model and the market are 12 or more points apart (3 of today's 37 fixtures). The model only sees results and match statistics; the market also prices team news, injuries, transfers and squad quality. On our own settled record (3,799 match-result predictions, July–October 2026), when the model sat 10–15 points above the market it said 46%, the market said 35% and the result came in 36% of the time. Read a flagged row as “the model hasn't seen something” before you read it as “the market is wrong”.
How to read this. The big number is the model's probability; the small number beneath it is the market's price, shown so you can see the two side by side. Kick-off times are UK. Nothing here is a tip and nothing here is a value call — our own studies found the model beats the market on accuracy in these markets while still losing to the bookmaker's margin, so treat these as forecasts, not selections.
● marks a season opener. When either side has played fewer than three matches this season, the published number is pulled 25% toward the market, because early-season form is thin and the market knows more than we do. 0 of today's 37 fixtures are marked. Everything else is the model alone, from a model that carries no odds features at all.
