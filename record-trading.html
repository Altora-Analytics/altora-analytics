How the backtests were validated (and what they can't tell you)

  • 15 years of data (2011–2026), every strategy tested net of estimated spread costs.
  • Every edge must be profitable in both halves of the sample independently. An edge that only worked in one era is discarded, not averaged in.
  • Cost stress test: the FX book stays profitable with spreads doubled.
  • We rebuilt the indices book from scratch after finding inflated features in an early version. The numbers above are from the clean rebuild, which cost us ~0.1 Sharpe and is exactly why you can trust the rest.
  • What backtests can't prove: the future. That's what the live demo record below is for.
