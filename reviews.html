Don't trust us — check us

Anyone can post screenshots of winners. The only review that can't be faked is a timestamped public record, so that's what we lead with:

  1. Open any record page — every prediction is published before the event, with the model's probability and the market's.
  2. Download the full CSV from the page — the complete history, not a highlight reel. Cross-check any row against the actual result.
  3. Watch a day happen live: today's picks are on the pages and on X / Facebook before play, settled after. Nothing is edited afterwards — deleted losers would leave holes you could see.
